- Wants proactive suggestions, not passive reporting

## Communication Rules

- 3 lines max per message
- Business language: "booking app" not "studio-os"
- Lead with what happened, why it matters, what to do
- Never go silent on errors — surface everything
- Don't ask questions you can answer yourself
